# Third-party notices

## KubeTier

Ran's RBAC risk metadata is derived from the machine-readable permission and
built-in Role reference published by [KubeTier](https://kubetier.com/). KubeTier
is an independent project by Andreas Døhl and is not affiliated with Ran.

The repository contains lookup metadata, source links, and attribution only.
Descriptions, notes, attack chains, and escalation steps are not redistributed;
operators may generate a private local catalog from KubeTier's Markdown pages.
The generated catalog records its retrieval time, source ETag, checksum, and the
Kubernetes version against which KubeTier reports validating the data.
